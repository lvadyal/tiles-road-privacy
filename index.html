Privacy Policy for Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D

Privacy Policy • Updated October 26, 2026 • Policy version 2026-10-26.1
Andrii Bigun • vadyainspire@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

The game does not require an account or ask for your name, address, phone number or email. Your progress is saved on your device.
With your optional choices and any applicable partner consent, third-party services may collect device, application, analytics and advertising identifiers; device model; operating system and app version; language and region; screen and connection information; IP address and approximate country; installation, session, gameplay and progression events; ad impressions and interactions; consent signals; and diagnostics, crash or performance information where enabled. We do not intentionally collect precise location.

2. Your choices

Gameplay analytics, marketing measurement and advertising are separate optional choices, initially off. You can decline them and continue playing. Reading this policy is not consent.
Open Settings > Privacy & Data at any time to change or withdraw your choices. Withdrawal stops future collection through the relevant feature; it does not delete information already processed. Your advertising choice covers the ironSource and Unity Ads services described below. With Advertising off, the game does not initialize these services or request further ads; an already displayed ad may finish.
The Do not sell or share choice opts out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising. Turning off personalization does not necessarily remove permitted contextual ads or processing for security and fraud prevention. Device advertising settings and in-ad controls offer further choices.

3. Gameplay analytics — ByteBrew, GameAnalytics and AppMetrica

If you enable gameplay analytics, ByteBrew, GameAnalytics and AppMetrica (Yandex) measure sessions, gameplay, progression and engagement to help us improve the game and diagnose problems. They may process analytics or device identifiers, device/app information and events. ByteBrew may also process advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location according to its SDK configuration. ByteBrew states that its services are hosted and operated in the United States.
GameAnalytics uses a request's IP address to determine country and states in its Collection API documentation that it does not store that IP address on its servers. Advertising-ID tracking is disabled in this game's GameAnalytics configuration.
AppMetrica processes device/app information, analytics identifiers, IP address, sessions and level events (level number, time spent, failure reason where applicable, and days since the first launch recorded on this device). AppMetrica location tracking and advertising-identifier tracking are disabled. We do not request location permission for AppMetrica. An IP address may still indicate an approximate country or region. AppMetrica starts only after you enable gameplay analytics and its services are reachable; it is skipped for the launch if the availability check fails. Withdrawing analytics consent stops future event reporting and data sending.
AppMetrica / Yandex privacy: https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/
AppMetrica terms: https://yandex.com/legal/metrica_termsofuse/
ByteBrew privacy: https://docs.bytebrew.io/BBSettings/privacypolicy
GameAnalytics privacy: https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-faq
GameAnalytics Trust Center: https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust

4. Marketing measurement — AppsFlyer and Meta

If you enable marketing measurement and turn off Do not sell or share, AppsFlyer and the Meta/Facebook SDK measure installs, attribution, campaigns and app events. They may process installation and event data, device/app information, IP address and app/device or advertising identifiers according to their settings and policies.
Marketing measurement is paused while Do not sell or share is on. The game does not use Facebook Login and does not require a Facebook account.
AppsFlyer privacy: https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/services-privacy-policy/
Meta privacy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

5. Advertising — Unity LevelPlay

If you enable advertising, Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) manages third-party ads, including reward videos and interstitial ads. The current build includes ironSource and Unity Ads. Other networks require a policy and consent-configuration update before being added.
Advertising services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device/app information, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, frequency management, fraud prevention and personalization where permitted. Consent signals are passed to supported partners. Ad availability and personalization depend on your region, choices and partner settings.
The in-game Advertising choice permits ironSource and Unity Ads to access device information for ad delivery and measurement. These services start only after that choice is saved. The game sends consent and Do not sell or share signals directly to LevelPlay; it does not use a separate consent-platform form. Personalization is allowed only when Advertising is on and Do not sell or share is off.
Unity player privacy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

6. How we use information

Depending on your choices, information supports gameplay analysis, installation attribution, campaign measurement, ad delivery and measurement, diagnostics, stability, performance, security, fraud prevention and improvements to gameplay. Local saves and requested passenger reminders support the game on your device.

7. Sharing

Data may be transmitted to enabled providers: ByteBrew, GameAnalytics, AppMetrica (Yandex), AppsFlyer, Meta, Unity LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads. Providers act on our behalf or under their own privacy terms, depending on the service.
Authorized publishers, contractors or business partners may receive controlled access to relevant analytics dashboards for operating, analyzing, marketing or improving the game. They do not receive direct access to your device through dashboard access.
We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising disclosures can qualify as sale, sharing or targeted advertising under some laws; use the Do not sell or share control or contact us to exercise applicable rights.

8. Security and retention

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. Providers apply their own measures, and supported SDK connections use encryption where supported.
Information is retained according to provider policies and configured retention periods, only as reasonably needed for the purposes above, security and legal obligations. Local game progress remains on your device until removed or reset. Privacy preferences and a local first-launch date are kept separately so resetting progress does not grant consent or reset the analytics installation age.

9. Your rights and deletion requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or receive your data, withdraw consent, restrict processing, object, or opt out of sale/sharing or targeted advertising. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.
Email vadyainspire@gmail.com with the game name, your request and any relevant installation or attribution details available to you. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. We will review your request and, where technically possible, delete or request deletion from the relevant providers.
You may also contact providers using the privacy pages above. Resetting an advertising ID or game progress does not itself delete data previously collected by providers. Anonymous, aggregated, security-related or legally required information may be retained where permitted.

10. Notifications

Passenger reminders are local and optional. Phone permission is requested only after you choose Track for a passenger. Settings > Privacy & Data > Notification settings lets you stop scheduled reminders and open the phone's notification controls. We do not request permission on first launch.

11. Children's privacy

The game is intended for players aged 16 and older and is not directed to children under 16. Its content rating does not change its intended audience. We do not knowingly solicit directly identifying information from children. Contact us if you believe a child's information has been collected inappropriately.

12. International processing

Providers may process information outside your country, under their privacy practices and applicable legal safeguards. See the provider policies for processing locations and safeguards.

13. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, providers or requirements change, showing a revised date and requesting fresh choices when necessary. Version 2026-10-26.1 adds AppMetrica gameplay analytics with location tracking disabled. Players who saved choices under an older policy must review this update and save fresh choices before optional services resume.
