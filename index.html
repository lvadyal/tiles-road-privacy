Privacy Policy for Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Andrii Bigun ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the game, how it may be used, how it may be shared, and what choices you have regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D does not require users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, postal address, phone number, or email address.

However, the game uses third-party analytics and attribution services that may automatically collect technical and usage information.

Information may include:

  • device and application identifiers;
  • advertising or attribution identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • device model and manufacturer;
  • operating system and version;
  • application version;
  • language and regional settings;
  • technical device information;
  • game launch and session information;
  • gameplay and application interaction events;
  • installation and attribution information;
  • crash reports;
  • diagnostic information;
  • application performance information.

We do not intentionally collect precise location information.

2. Yandex AppMetrica

Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D uses Yandex AppMetrica for analytics, application performance measurement, crash reporting, diagnostics, and attribution.

AppMetrica may process technical information about the device and application, application usage data, diagnostic data, crash information, and device or application identifiers.

We use this information to understand how the game is used, analyze gameplay and user interaction, measure installations and attribution, identify crashes and technical problems, improve application stability and performance, and improve gameplay and user experience.

AppMetrica is provided by Yandex and processes information according to its own privacy terms and policies.

3. Meta / Facebook SDK

Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D uses the Meta/Facebook SDK for installation attribution, campaign measurement, and application analytics.

The Meta/Facebook SDK may process technical information and device or application identifiers to determine whether an installation or application event is associated with a Meta advertising campaign or other attribution source.

The game does not use Facebook Login and does not require a Facebook account.

Meta processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected through the game and third-party SDKs may be used for application analytics, gameplay analytics, measuring installations and attribution, marketing campaign measurement, understanding application usage, crash reporting and diagnostics, improving application stability and performance, fraud prevention and security, and improving gameplay and user experience.

5. Data Sharing

Some data may be transmitted to third-party service providers used by the game, including Yandex AppMetrica and Meta Platforms / Facebook.

These providers may process information on our behalf or according to their own privacy policies.

We may also grant authorized business partners, publishers, contractors, or service providers access to analytics dashboards for purposes such as campaign measurement, marketing performance analysis, product analytics, gameplay analysis, application performance analysis, and product improvement.

Such access is provided through controlled account permissions and is limited to the purposes necessary for operating, analyzing, marketing, or improving the game.

These authorized partners do not receive direct access to your device and do not independently collect data from the application unless separately disclosed in this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell users’ personal information.

6. Advertising

The current version of Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D does not display third-party advertising inside the game.

However, device or application identifiers may be used for installation attribution and marketing campaign measurement, including measuring whether an installation resulted from an advertising campaign.

If advertising is introduced into the game in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed in connection with the game.

Third-party providers used by the application also apply their own technical and organizational security measures.

Data transmitted through supported third-party SDKs is generally transmitted using encrypted network connections where supported by those services.

8. Data Retention

Information collected through third-party SDKs may be retained according to the retention policies and configuration of the respective service providers.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for analytics, attribution, diagnostics, application improvement, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other legitimate purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

9. Data Deletion Requests

If you want to request deletion of data associated with your use of Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D, please contact:

vadyainspire@gmail.com

In your message, please include:

  • the application name: Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D;
  • a clear statement that you are requesting deletion of data associated with the application;
  • any relevant device, installation, or attribution information available to you, if applicable.

We will review the request and, where technically possible, delete or request deletion of data associated with the application from the analytics and attribution services we use, including Yandex AppMetrica and Meta/Facebook.

Removing Meta/Facebook App Activity

You may also remove app activity associated with Meta/Facebook by following these steps:

  1. Open your Facebook account and go to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D.
  4. Select the app and choose Remove.

Please note that some anonymized, aggregated, security-related, or legally required information may be retained where permitted by law or where it can no longer reasonably be associated with an individual user.

If you have any questions about data deletion, contact vadyainspire@gmail.com.

If you believe that personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us at vadyainspire@gmail.com.
