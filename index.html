Privacy Policy for Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Andrii Bigun ("we", "our", or "us") operates the mobile game Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the game, how it may be used, how it may be shared, and what choices you have regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D does not require users to create an account and does not directly request personal information such as your name, postal address, phone number, or email address.

However, the game uses third-party analytics, attribution, advertising, and ad mediation services that may automatically collect technical, usage, and advertising information.

The information processed depends on the service, SDK configuration, enabled features, and applicable permissions or consent.

Information may include:

  • device, application, and analytics user identifiers;
  • advertising or attribution identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • device model and manufacturer;
  • operating system and version;
  • application version;
  • language and regional settings;
  • technical device information, including screen resolution;
  • connection type;
  • IP address;
  • approximate location, such as country, derived from IP address or regional information;
  • game launch and session information;
  • gameplay and application interaction events;
  • installation and attribution information;
  • advertising activity, such as ad impressions, clicks, and video interactions;
  • advertising consent and privacy preference signals, where provided;
  • crash reports;
  • diagnostic information;
  • application performance information.

We do not intentionally collect precise location information.

2. Yandex AppMetrica

Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D uses Yandex AppMetrica for analytics, application performance measurement, crash reporting, diagnostics, and attribution.

AppMetrica may process technical information about the device and application, application usage data, diagnostic data, crash information, and device or application identifiers.

We use this information to understand how the game is used, analyze gameplay and user interaction, measure installations and attribution, identify crashes and technical problems, improve application stability and performance, and improve gameplay and user experience.

AppMetrica is provided by Yandex and processes information according to its own privacy terms and policies.

3. Meta / Facebook SDK

Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D uses the Meta/Facebook SDK for installation attribution, campaign measurement, and application analytics.

The Meta/Facebook SDK may process technical information and device or application identifiers to determine whether an installation or application event is associated with a Meta advertising campaign or other attribution source.

The game does not use Facebook Login and does not require a Facebook account.

Meta processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

4. ByteBrew

Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D uses ByteBrew for gameplay analytics, measuring sessions and player engagement, and improving the player experience.

Depending on the SDK configuration, ByteBrew may process user identifiers, device information, operating system version, language, screen resolution, connection type, IP address, approximate location, session timestamps, and gameplay or progression events sent by the game. Advertising identifiers may also be processed where collection is enabled and permitted by applicable settings and requirements.

ByteBrew processes this information on our behalf to provide its services. ByteBrew states that its services are hosted and operated in the United States.

For more information, see the ByteBrew Privacy Policy.

5. GameAnalytics

Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D uses GameAnalytics to analyze game usage, sessions, player engagement, and progression, and to improve gameplay and user experience.

Depending on the SDK configuration and events sent by the game, GameAnalytics may process an analytics user identifier, device and application information, operating system version, session information, gameplay and progression events, and error events. Advertising identifiers may also be processed where their collection is enabled and permitted by applicable settings and requirements.

GameAnalytics uses the IP address of a request to determine the country. Its Collection API documentation states that the IP address is not stored on its servers.

GameAnalytics processes player data on our behalf to provide analytics services. For more information about its handling of player data, see the GameAnalytics Developer Policy and Trust Center.

6. How We Use Information

Information collected through the game and third-party SDKs may be used for application analytics, gameplay analytics, measuring installations and attribution, marketing campaign measurement, understanding application usage, crash reporting and diagnostics, improving application stability and performance, fraud prevention and security, and improving gameplay and user experience.

Advertising information may also be used to select and deliver ads, operate ad mediation, measure ad performance, manage ad frequency, prevent invalid traffic, and personalize ads where permitted and subject to applicable consent requirements.

7. Data Sharing

Some data may be transmitted to third-party service providers used by the game, including Yandex AppMetrica, Meta Platforms / Facebook, ByteBrew, GameAnalytics, and Unity LevelPlay (ironSource). Advertising data may also be shared with advertising networks and demand partners participating in the game's configured mediation setup.

These providers may process information on our behalf or according to their own privacy policies.

We may also grant authorized business partners, publishers, contractors, or service providers access to analytics dashboards for purposes such as campaign measurement, marketing performance analysis, product analytics, gameplay analysis, application performance analysis, and product improvement.

Such access is provided through controlled account permissions and is limited to the purposes necessary for operating, analyzing, marketing, or improving the game.

These authorized partners do not receive direct access to your device and do not independently collect data from the application unless separately disclosed in this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising-related disclosures may qualify as a "sale," "sharing," or processing for targeted advertising under some privacy laws. Applicable advertising privacy choices are described in Section 8.

8. Advertising — Unity LevelPlay

Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) to display and manage third-party advertising. LevelPlay is an ad mediation platform: ads may be supplied by Unity Ads, ironSource Ads, or other advertising networks enabled in the game's mediation setup. The ad formats and providers used depend on the game's configuration; not every network supported by LevelPlay is used by the game.

Advertising Data

Depending on the SDKs, enabled networks, device settings, and applicable consent, advertising services may process advertising and device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, device and application information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. This data supports ad delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Consent and privacy preference signals may be passed to supported advertising partners.

Personalized Ads and Your Choices

Ads may be contextual or personalized, depending on your region, privacy choices, and the enabled advertising services. Consent is required before processing that depends on consent under applicable law; viewing or accepting this Privacy Policy alone does not provide that consent.

You may use available device advertising settings, in-ad privacy controls, or in-game consent controls, where provided, to manage relevant choices. Opting out of personalization does not necessarily remove ads or stop data processing needed for permitted contextual advertising, security, and fraud prevention.

You may contact us at vadyainspire@gmail.com to withdraw consent or exercise applicable rights, including opting out of targeted advertising or the sale or sharing of personal information. Requests concerning data handled independently by an advertising provider may also be submitted using that provider's privacy contact or controls.

For details about Unity's handling of player data and privacy rights, see the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Other advertising networks process data according to their own policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed in connection with the game.

Third-party providers used by the application also apply their own technical and organizational security measures.

Data transmitted through supported third-party SDKs is generally transmitted using encrypted network connections where supported by those services.

10. Data Retention

Information collected through Yandex AppMetrica, Meta/Facebook, ByteBrew, GameAnalytics, Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), and participating advertising partners may be retained according to the retention policies and configuration of the respective providers.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for analytics, attribution, advertising delivery and measurement, diagnostics, application improvement, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other legitimate purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

  2. Open Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Tiles Road: Taxi Puzzle 3D.
  4. Select the app and choose Remove.

If you have any questions about data deletion, contact vadyainspire@gmail.com.
